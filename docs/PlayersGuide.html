Getting Started

Welcome to Tootsville

Tootsville is a magical 3D virtual world where you can explore, make friends, and embark on adventures as a colorful Toot (bipedal elephant). This guide will help you navigate the world and understand all the features available to you.

Sign Up & Account Creation

Creating Your Account

Welcome to Tootsville! To begin your adventure:

  1. Visit tootsville.org
  2. Click "Register" or "Sign Up" to create a new account
  3. Choose a unique username and secure password
  4. Verify your email address
  5. Complete the character creation process
  6. Log in and start exploring!

Authentication Options

Tootsville supports multiple sign-in methods:

  • Google Sign-In: Quick and secure sign-in with your Google account
  • Yahoo! Sign-In: Use your Yahoo! account for authentication
  • Apple Sign-In: Secure sign-in with Apple ID (iOS/macOS users)
  • Bluesky Sign-In: Connect with your Bluesky social account
  • Traditional Account: Create a username/password account

Child Account System

For players under 13 years old, Tootsville provides a special child account system:

  • Parental Consent: Requires parent/guardian approval
  • Diamond Icon: Child accounts display a diamond icon for safety
  • Restricted Features: Limited access to certain social features
  • Enhanced Safety: Additional content filtering and monitoring
  • Waiting for Parents Mode: Temporary access while awaiting parental approval

Character Creation

After signing up, you'll create your Toot character:

  1. Choose Your Name: Select a unique name for your Toot
  2. Customize Appearance: Pick colors, patterns, and accessories
  3. Select Starting Location: Choose where to begin your adventure
  4. Set Preferences: Configure audio, graphics, and control settings

User Interface Guide

Main Game Interface

The Tootsville interface is designed to be intuitive and accessible:

Screen Layout

  • 3D World View: The main area showing the game world
  • Top Menu Bar: Contains the T-menu (settings), help, and system options
  • Bottom Chat Area: Text input and communication controls
  • Side Panels: Inventory, character info, and other tools
  • Status Indicators: Health, energy, and connection status

Menu System

  • T-Menu (Top-Left): Main settings and system options
  • Paperdoll (F2): Character customization and equipment
  • Mobile (F3): Phone-like interface with contacts and apps
  • Talk Box (F4): Enhanced chat and communication
  • Contacts (F6): Friend list and messaging
  • Camera (F9): Screenshot and photo capture
  • Control Panel (F10): Advanced settings and preferences

Status Indicators

  • Diamond Icon: Indicates child/sensitive player accounts
  • Connection Status: Shows server connection quality
  • Audio Status: Indicates microphone and speaker status
  • Notification Badges: Show unread messages and alerts

Game Controls

Movement Controls

You can navigate Tootanga in several ways:

Supported Input Methods

  • Mouse Movement: Click anywhere to walk to that location
  • Arrow Keys: Move in the direction you're facing
  • Gamepad: Use connected game controllers
  • Touch Controls: Mobile devices support touch navigation
  • Remote Control: TV platforms support remote navigation

Important Note: Movement Restrictions

Elephants don't jump! Toots (being elephants) cannot jump. This is an intentional design choice that reflects the nature of elephants.

  • Toots: Can walk, run, sit, and swim, but cannot jump
  • Birds/Katootels: Can fly, jump, walk, run, and sit
  • Manatees: Can only swim, cannot walk, run, jump, or fly
  • Other Characters: Have default movement capabilities

Keyboard Shortcuts

Essential keyboard shortcuts for efficient gameplay:

Control Panel
  • Open Control Panel: F10, Menu, or Ctrl/Cmd + PageUp
  • Music Volume: Ctrl/Cmd + Arrow Up/Down
  • Help: F1 or Help key
Mobile Interface
  • Open Mobile: Page Down, Alt/Meta + M, or F3
  • Contacts: Ctrl/Cmd + Page Down, or F6
  • Camera: Shift + Page Down, Print, or F9
Communication
  • Open Talk Box: Home, Ctrl/Cmd + A, End, Ctrl/Cmd + E, or F4
  • Hide Talk Box: Ctrl/Cmd + Home or Alt/Meta + H
  • SHOUT: Ctrl/Cmd + , (comma)
  • Normal Speech: Ctrl/Cmd + / (slash) or Ctrl/Cmd + ' (apostrophe)
  • Whisper: Ctrl/Cmd + . (period)
Character Management
  • Paperdoll: Page Up, F2, or Alt/Meta + P
  • Use Equipped Item: Shift + Home or hold Shift + click
  • Switch Items: Shift + Page Up or F7

World Lore & History

The World of Tootanga

Tootanga is a magical island where the impossible becomes possible through the power of wish magic.

The Tootangan Wish Mist

The Tootangan Wish Mist is a mysterious magical substance that has the power to transform reality. It was discovered by the original elephants who would become the Basic 8 Toots.

The Origin Story

Long ago, eight regular elephants discovered the Tootangan Wish Mist and were magically transformed into the first Toots - colorful, intelligent, bipedal elephants. These became known as the Basic 8.

The Basic 8

The Basic 8 are the original Toots who discovered the wish mist:

  • Zap: An energetic, electric character with lightning bolt patterns
  • Flora: A nature-loving character with plant-themed abilities
  • Sparkle: A magical character with sparkly effects and powers
  • Moo: A character with cow-like patterns and gentle personality
  • Superstar: A celebrity character with fame and charisma
  • Lil MC: A music and entertainment character
  • Cupid: Associated with love, friendship, and romance
  • Dottie: A character with distinctive polka-dot patterns

The Wish for Friends

After their transformation, the Basic 8 wished for more friends. This wish magic brought all the other characters (except Pil and Doodle) into existence, creating the diverse population of Tootsville.

Special Characters

Some characters have unique origins and abilities:

  • Pil: The only Toot who existed before the Basic 8. She is child-like but has immense magical powers and is essentially the embodiment of the system "root" account. Her name means "Elephant" in ancient Hebrew.
  • Doodle: Like the Basic 8, Doodle was once a regular elephant who discovered the Tootangan Wish Mist. He was originally plain white but got his name because he loved to paint patterns on himself. He can use wish magic to turn art into new patterns.
  • Bella, the Tiniest Toot Fairy: A magical fairy character with special abilities

Magical Creatures

  • Katootels: Colorful giant bird pets that follow Toots around
  • Gossip Mice: Led by Little Reminder, they carry news and mail between players
  • Mist Parrots: Parrot Nick and Parrot Jodie greet new players and guide them through the game
  • Dragón: A friendly dragon at Castillo Toot who teaches Spanish phrases

World Locations

Major Locations in Tootanga

Tootanga is filled with diverse and magical locations to explore:

Grand Toot Station

The central transportation hub of Tootanga. This is the main transit center where you can access trains to all other locations in the world. You can reach Grand Toot Station from any train station, making it the perfect starting point for exploration.

Big Tootoona Beach

A beautiful beach area featuring:

  • Surfing: Ride the waves on the beach
  • Boat Tours: Explore the Bay of Elphelas by boat
  • Water Sports: Various aquatic activities
  • Beach Volleyball: Play volleyball on the sand
  • Raving Burger: A popular food establishment
  • Access: Via train at Big Tootoona Station

Castillo Toot

An ancient castle on Greffle Point offering:

  • Dragón: Meet the friendly dragon and learn Spanish phrases
  • Jousting Field: Participate in medieval sports
  • Horseback Riding: Ride horses on the cliffs of Greffle Point
  • Castillo Stables: Access point for horseback activities
  • Underground Station: Castillo Station for return to modern civilization

Transit System

Tootanga features a comprehensive train network:

  • Grand Toot Station: Central hub connecting all locations
  • Big Tootoona Station: Access to the beach area
  • Castillo Station: Underground station at the castle
  • Multiple Routes: Various train lines connecting different areas
  • Seamless Travel: Easy transportation between all major locations

Other Notable Areas

  • Art Studios: Where you can create with Doodle and Picasso
  • Music Venues: Locations for musical performances and activities
  • Nature Areas: Forests, gardens, and natural spaces
  • Urban Areas: City-like environments with shops and activities

Character Guide

What Are Toots?

In Tootsville, you play as a Toot - a colorful, intelligent, bipedal elephant.

  • Pronunciation: "Toots" rhymes with "boots" (not like when a gangster in an old movie says, "Hey, toots, how are you?")
  • Appearance: Colorful, intelligent, bipedal elephants with unique personalities
  • Origin: The Basic 8 Toots were actual elephants who were magically transformed by Tootangan Wish Mist into their cartoon forms
  • Population: All other Toots were created by wish magic when the Basic 8 wished for more friends

Character Types

  • Toots (Elephants): The main character type - cannot jump but can walk, run, sit, and swim
  • Birds/Katootels: Can fly, jump, walk, run, and sit

Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

If you encounter problems, try these solutions:

Connection Issues

  • Check Internet: Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Browser Compatibility: Use a supported web browser
  • Clear Cache: Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Firewall Settings: Check if firewall is blocking the connection

Performance Issues

  • Graphics Settings: Lower graphics quality in settings
  • Close Other Apps: Free up system resources
  • Update Drivers: Ensure graphics drivers are up to date
  • Browser Updates: Update to the latest browser version

Audio Problems

  • Check Volume: Ensure system and browser volume are up
  • Microphone Permissions: Allow microphone access in browser
  • Audio Settings: Check in-game audio settings
  • Device Selection: Select correct audio input/output devices
