Getting Started

What Are Toots?

In Tootsville, you play as a Toot - a colorful, intelligent, bipedal elephant.

  • Pronunciation: "Toots" rhymes with "boots" (not like when a gangster in an old movie says, "Hey, toots, how are you?")
  • Appearance: Colorful, intelligent, bipedal elephants with unique personalities
  • Origin: The Basic 8 Toots were actual elephants who were magically transformed by Tootangan Wish Mist into their cartoon forms
  • Population: All other Toots were created by wish magic when the Basic 8 wished for more friends

Katootels

Katootels are colorful giant bird pets that follow around Toots (elephants). These friendly companions add to the magical atmosphere of Tootsville and can be found throughout the game world.

Gossip Mice

Throughout Tootanga are Gossip Mice, led by Little Reminder. These mice carry news and mail between players, serving as the communication network of the game world.

Mist Parrots

Parrot Nick and Parrot Jodie greet new players when they join the game. They represent the player membership software and guide players through their initial experience in Tootsville, including character creation and tutorial content.

Creating Your Account

Welcome to Tootsville! To begin your adventure:

  1. Visit tootsville.org
  2. Click "Register" to create a new account
  3. Choose a unique username and secure password
  4. Verify your email address
  5. Log in and start exploring!

System Requirements

To enjoy Tootsville, you'll need:

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
  • JavaScript enabled
  • WebGL support for 3D graphics
  • A stable internet connection
  • At least 2GB of RAM

First Steps

When you first enter Tootsville:

  1. You'll appear in the Welcome Plaza
  2. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the controls
  3. Look for other players to interact with
  4. Explore the immediate area
  5. Check your inventory for starter items

Controls

System Requirements

You'll need a mouse (or other pointing device, such as a pen or touchscreen) and keyboard to play. If you want to play on a mobile device that doesn't have a built-in keyboard, we strongly recommend using a BlueTooth or other external keyboard.

Screen Layout

There are five objects around the edges of your screen:

  • Top-left: Control Panel (with the Tootsville T logo)
  • Bottom-left: Your Toot's mobile
  • Bottom-center: Talk Box
  • Bottom-right: Your Toot's paperdoll
  • Top-right: Item you're holding in your trunk (if any)

Gameplay

Exploring the World

Tootsville is a vast 3D world filled with:

  • Plazas and Gathering Areas: Meet other players
  • Adventure Zones: Explore mysterious locations
  • Shops and Markets: Trade and purchase items
  • Hidden Areas: Discover secret locations
  • Seasonal Events: Participate in special activities

Getting Around in Tootanga

There are several ways to explore the island:

Walking

You can explore the entire un-misted portion of the island just by walking. This is the most basic and reliable way to get around.

Train Travel

Take the train for faster travel between destinations. There are train stations throughout the island, and you can still enjoy the countryside during your journey.

Train Lines
South Line (Currently Operating)
  • Grand Toot Station in Tootsville (city)
  • Looking-Glass Falls Station near Looking-Glass Falls
  • Big Tootoona Station near Big Tootoona Beach
  • Taft Landing Station in Taft Landing
  • Castillo Station under Castillo Toot
  • Brunner Station in Cape Brunner
West Line

The West Line is not running yet.

North Line

The North Line is not running yet.

Vehicles

Buy and use various vehicles to get around more quickly:

  • Land Vehicles: Cars, horses, bicycles, skateboards
  • Water Vehicles: Boats, fishing boats, tall ships, submarines
  • Air Vehicles: Hang gliders, flying saucers, moon rockets
  • Special Vehicles: Surfboards, trains

Note: Wheelchairs are technically vehicles but are allowed pretty much everywhere, while other vehicles have restricted access in certain areas.

Riding Locations

You can ride horseback from:

  • Castillo Stables: Ride on the trails of Greffle Point
  • Big T Ranch: Ride on the Tootengessi Savannah

Mist Limit

Some areas of the island are covered in mist and may have travel restrictions. See the Mist Limit section for more details about accessing these areas.

Neighborhoods and Housing

Players can build homes in various themed neighborhoods throughout Tootsville:

Available Neighborhoods

  • City Limits: Urban-themed residential area
  • Tootlantis: Underwater-themed neighborhood
  • Big Tootoona: Beach and tropical-themed area
  • Comic World: Comic book and superhero-themed neighborhood
  • Dino Land: Prehistoric and dinosaur-themed area
  • Enchanted Forest: Magical forest-themed neighborhood
  • Moonbase T: Space and lunar-themed area
  • Pirates' Cove: Pirate and nautical-themed neighborhood
  • Toot Castle: Medieval castle-themed area
  • Toot Safari: African safari-themed neighborhood

Each neighborhood has its own unique atmosphere, decorative themes, and community feel. Choose the one that best matches your style and interests!

Address System

Tootsville uses a structured address system for all homes and buildings:

  • Streets: Run East/West
  • Avenues: Run North/South
  • Address Format: [House Number] [Direction] [Street/Avenue Number] [St/Ave]

Example addresses:

  • 1112 NW 16th St - House #1112 on Northwest 16th Street
  • 1612 NW 11th Ave - House #1612 on Northwest 11th Avenue

This system makes it easy to navigate and find specific locations within each neighborhood.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

Game Won't Load

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Clear your browser cache
  • Update your browser to the latest version

Poor Performance

  • Close other browser tabs
  • Reduce graphics quality in settings
  • Check if your device meets minimum requirements
  • Try a different browser

Can't Connect to Server

  • Check if the server is online
  • Verify your internet connection
  • Try connecting to a different server
  • Contact support if the issue persists
